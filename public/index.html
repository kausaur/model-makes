Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our finished models, custom orders, and delivery.

Are the models delivered fully assembled and finished? +
Yes, absolutely! Every model arrives completely assembled, hand-finished, and ready to display or use right out of the box. No painting, gluing, or preparation is required.
Can I request custom colors or paint finishes? +
Yes! If you would like a specific color palette, metallic sheen, or custom painted detailing, simply reach out to us on WhatsApp before ordering and we will gladly personalize it for you.
How should I clean and care for my finished model? +
Our models are sealed with protective finishes. For routine dusting, simply wipe gently with a soft dry microfiber cloth or a soft-bristle brush. To preserve vibrant paint tones, keep models away from excessive direct heat and harsh outdoor sunlight.
How long does shipping take? +
In-stock models ship within 24 to 48 hours. Custom requested finishes take 2-3 days for detailing and curing. Pan-India courier delivery typically takes 5-7 business days depending on your pin code.
What if my model arrives damaged during transit? +
We provide a 100% Transit Guarantee. Every piece is cushioned with multi-layer shock-absorbing wrapping and rigid cartons. In the rare event of damage, just share an unboxing photo/video on WhatsApp and we will dispatch a replacement immediately.
Do you offer bulk/wholesale pricing? +
Yes! For corporate gifting, event merchandise, architectural models, or board game component manufacturing, contact us via WhatsApp for tiered wholesale volume pricing.

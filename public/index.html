About Node 'n' Nozzle

Node 'n' Nozzle is an independent maker studio based in India, specializing in finished display pieces, tactile mechanical gadgets, and collectible pop-culture art. We bridge state-of-the-art 3D manufacturing with meticulous post-processing and hand-finishing.

Unlike raw unpolished 3D prints, every piece from our studio arrives completely assembled, hand-smoothed, cured, and protected with durable satin or matte coatings. Whether it's an articulated wyvern for your workspace or a telescoping samurai blade, your piece is built to be admired and handled every day.
